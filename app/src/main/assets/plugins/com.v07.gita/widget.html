menu_book GITA 2.47
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन। मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥
"You have a right to perform your duty, but not to the fruits of action. Never let results be your motive, nor be attached to inaction."
