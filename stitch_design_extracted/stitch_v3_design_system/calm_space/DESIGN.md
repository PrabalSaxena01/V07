## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intimacy, stillness, and tactile clarity of opening a finely bound personal notebook. It rejects the visual mechanics of productivity dashboards, enterprise consoles, and dense administrative tools in favor of an unhurried, editorial sanctuary for self-reflection and life logging.

The emotional signature is quiet, grounding, and organic:
- **Paper Metaphor:** Surfaces interact as sheets of natural, warm-tinted vellum layered over soft stone rather than glass or synthetic plastic.
- **Editorial Presence:** Generous breathing room, deliberate typographic pacing, and sentence-case headers set an unhurried cadence.
- **Organic Warmth:** Earth pigments—calm sage leaf, dried terracotta clay, and deep charcoal inks—replace sterile digital primaries and saturated alerts.
- **Tactile Softness:** Generous contours, diffuse warm ambient diffusion, and subtle borders create an analog, touch-friendly presence that prioritizes peace of mind over data density.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from tactile writing instruments and natural substrates:

- **Canvas & Background (`#F6F7F4`):** A warm, tinted paper tone that acts as the foundational sheet. Never pure #FFFFFF.
- **Surfaces:**
  - `surface` (`#FFFFFF`): Clean paper cards, crisp leaf insertions, and focused writing planes.
  - `surface-soft` (`#EEF2EE`): Tinted secondary fills, recessed note trays, and gentle grouped containers.
- **Typography & Ink:**
  - `text-primary` (`#202824`): Deep ink with a hint of dark pine, delivering high legibility without harsh synthetic contrast.
  - `text-secondary` (`#66716B`): Soft charcoal and medium pencil for body support, timestamps, and secondary reflections.
  - `text-tertiary` (`#8A948E`): Light pencil margins, subdued counters, and subtle metadata.
- **Accents:**
  - `accent` (`#397A68`): Forest leaf green and calm sage for focal actions, active states, and quiet progress markers.
  - `accent-soft` (`#E3F0EB`): Morning mist wash for badges, soft chip backgrounds, and highlighted quotes.
  - `warm` (`#A86F61`): Earthy terracotta and dried clay for emotional milestones, evening entries, and warmth anchors.
  - `warm-soft` (`#F4E9E5`): Warm clay wash for gentle warnings, memory markers, and soft focal moments.
- **Structural Lines:**
  - `border` (`#E1E7E2`): Crisp, quiet outlines akin to ruled journal lines.
  - `divider` (`#EDF1EE`): Whisper-light structural breaks.
- **States:**
  - `danger` (`#B84A4A`): Muted dried madder red for intentional destructive actions, avoiding loud digital alarm tones.
  - `danger-soft` (`#F8E8E7`): Gentle blush wash for error containment.

## Typography

Typography is treated with literary restraint and natural speech patterns.

- **Humanist Pacing:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` delivers a gentle geometry with soft, organic curves. Avoid all mechanical rigidities.
- **Strictly Sentence Case:** All titles, headers, chips, navigation triggers, and button states must use natural sentence case. Never employ uppercase tracking or loud capital letters.
- **Generous Line Height:** Body text features expanded vertical rhythm (line height 1.6x–1.65x) to accommodate relaxed reading and uninterrupted writing flows.
- **Restricted Monospace:** Monospace type is prohibited across all standard UI elements, dates, indices, chips, and metrics. It is reserved exclusively for raw technical file pathways if mandated.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy prioritizes generous margins, open canvas space, and vertical cadence over dense grid-packing:

- **Single Column Dominance:** Layouts center around a focused reading column (maximum width 680px for entries; 840px for overview streams). Avoid multi-nested sidebars or dense dashboard splits.
- **Open Canvas Breathing:** Group elements via generous whitespace gaps (`space-lg` to `space-2xl`) rather than nesting containers within containers.
- **Breakpoints & Form Factors:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack with `1.25rem` (20px) outer edge margins, fluid full-width cards, and floating dock navigation anchored at the bottom edge.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** Single centered editorial rail with `2rem` outer margins. Secondary context or prompts appear in quiet inline panels rather than split screens.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** Centered layout with generous `3rem` side breathing room. Subtle auxiliary tools gently dock to the periphery with minimal visual weight.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold synthetic drop shadows or glossy translucent blurs. It relies instead on ambient light interacting with stacked paper surfaces:

- **Tonal Grounding:** The `#F6F7F4` paper canvas provides the baseline floor. Primary interactive content sits on `#FFFFFF` cards, giving immediate tactile differentiation without harsh outlines.
- **Warm Ambient Diffusion:**
  - *Resting Surfaces:* `box-shadow: 0 2px 12px -2px rgba(32, 40, 36, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(32, 40, 36, 0.02)`.
  - *Floating Elements (e.g., bottom nav bar, elevated action modals):* `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(32, 40, 36, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(32, 40, 36, 0.03)`.
- **Soft Paper Borders:** Surfaces are paired with a 1px solid border in `--border` (`#E1E7E2`), creating crisp, notebook-like edge definition that retains its warmth under varying display temperatures.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects warm, handheld stationery and smoothed pebbles:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Standardized at `20px` border-radius (`rounded-xl` equivalent in this system), providing an approachable, tactile container that softens the interface.
- **Buttons & Action Triggers:** Scaled between `12px` and full pill shapes (`9999px`) for quick tactile thumb targets.
- **Badges & Micro Elements:** Gently softened with `8px` to `12px` radii to avoid harsh clinical boxes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `--accent` (`#397A68`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `12px` border-radius or full pill (`9999px`), `12px 24px` padding. Subtle transform (`scale(0.98)`) and gentle darkening on press. No high-gloss gradients.
- **Secondary / Soft:** `--accent-soft` (`#E3F0EB`) background, `--accent` text. No border.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, `--text-secondary` text, softening to `--surface-soft` background on hover.
- **Warm Alternative:** Solid `--warm` (`#A86F61`) background with white text for contemplative or milestone milestones.

### Cards & Paper Sheets
- Background: `--surface` (`#FFFFFF`).
- Border: `1px solid var(--border)` (`#E1E7E2`).
- Border Radius: `20px`.
- Padding: `20px` to `24px`.
- Usage: Never nest white cards inside white cards. For grouping within cards, rely on subtle `--surface-soft` (`#EEF2EE`) field backgrounds or structural spacing.

### Input Fields & Writing Areas
- **Single-line Inputs:** `--surface` background, `1px solid var(--border)`, `12px` border-radius, `12px 16px` padding. Label rendered above in `--text-secondary` at `13px`. Focus state shifts the border cleanly to `--accent` with a zero-offset halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px var(--accent-soft)`.
- **Journal Canvas (Textarea):** Borderless, unadorned writing area directly on the card or paper canvas. Placeholder in `--text-tertiary` using gentle conversational prompts (e.g., "What brought you ease today?").

### Chips & Tags
- Height: `32px`.
- Shape: Full pill (`9999px`).
- Inactive: `--surface-soft` background, `--text-secondary` label, zero border.
- Active: `--accent-soft` background, `--accent` label, subtle `1px solid var(--accent)`.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Avoid heavy enclosed boxes. Present list rows separated by `1px solid var(--divider)` (`#EDF1EE`).
- Generous vertical padding (`16px 8px`).
- Trailing metadata (time, status) rendered in `--text-tertiary` using `body-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes: `20px × 20px`, `6px` soft rounded square. Unchecked has `1.5px solid var(--border)`. Checked fills with `--accent` displaying a soft organic white checkmark.
- Radio Buttons: `20px × 20px` circle with a centered `8px` solid `--accent` dot on checked state.

### Floating Navigation Bar
- Position: Anchored bottom center, elevated with `16px` clearance from screen edges.
- Background: `--surface` (`#FFFFFF`) with diffuse warm floating shadow.
- Border: `1px solid var(--border)`.
- Shape: Pill radius (`9999px`).
- Destinations: Exactly 5 quiet nodes: **Home**, **Tools**, **Journal**, **Day**, **You**.
- Items: Soft vertical icon + micro label stack. Active item is signaled by `--accent` tint and subtle underdot; inactive items remain in `--text-secondary`.
